## Review focus

1. Send succeeds but response is lost; composer closes, reopens or switches view before retry. Original request remains bound to immutable payload and exactly one message is created (Task 1).
2. Membership or device revoked, same person re-joins later, or a different account/room opens. No cached text, retry payload, attachment preview, business reference or preference can leak across scopes (Tasks 1–3).
3. Concurrent workers, two approved devices, malformed or forged identity, repeated requests; rate budgets cannot be bypassed through untrusted person IDs or worker-local resets, and denied operations do not perform expensive writes (Task 4).
4. Claim commits in Supabase but Firebase send does not; cleanup competes with retry. Never free capacity/delete bytes still referenced or able to be committed by an in-flight sender (Task 5).
5. A cached open tab remains active during emergency stop; recovery involves both stores. Stop must close all relevant write/read paths covered by the stop contract, not just hide UI, and restore must preserve identity/authorization and verify hashes (Task 6).
